Well-Being Ring / Well-Being Design Series / Episode 05

The Evolution.

A stressed person can follow only a few steps. Every extra screen costs energy.

This episode maps the journey from first launch to a calm home, then shows the user flow, the key screens, and the Material 3 system and components that hold them together.

01  Journey map

From first launch to a calm home.

(Method: journey map built from the prototype flow; to be tested with 5 to 8 people who feel stressed regularly)

The journey has two parts. Setup happens once. The main loop happens every day. Each stage shows what the user does, what the design intends, and the risk to watch.

01

Onboarding

What the user does

Reads the welcome and a short explanation, then grants permissions.

Design intent

Say what the app does and what it doesn't: wellbeing support, not a medical device.

Watch: a long permissions list before the user has seen any value.

02

Ring pairing

What the user does

Connects the ring and runs a quick calibration.

Design intent

One step on screen at a time, with clear progress.

Watch: calibration that feels like a test.

03

Emergency contact and consent

What the user does

Adds a trusted contact and sets data consent.

Design intent

Opt-in, item by item, and easy to revoke.

Watch: consent buried inside settings.

04

Home

What the user does

Sees one orb state and one main button.

Design intent

Decide for the user, and keep the screen quiet.

Watch: the screen grows with every new feature.

05

Stress moment

What the user does

Taps the main button: breathing for Rising or Stressed, emergency help for Critical.

Design intent

No menu. Breathing is paced by live heart rate.

Watch: breathing that adapts too slowly to help.

06

Result and follow-up

What the user does

Sees a before and after result, then goes Home or breathes again. If still tense, a counselor is offered first.

Design intent

Show whether the action worked, or bring in a person when it didn't.

Watch: a loop of "try again" with no way forward.

Colour, type, icons and motion, in one set.

(Source: prototype tokens on Material Design 3 dark theme)

Google's four colours carry the stress states, used as dark-theme tints. Colour is never the only signal: every state also has a word label.

Type scale (proposed)

Title · 16 / 24 · MediumTake a two-minute pause
Body · 14 / 20 · RegularBreathe with the orb whenever you're ready. You can stop at any time.

Roboto, with Google Sans where it is available. Body text is 14 pixels on a 20 pixel line.

Motion

ElementWhat moves, and why
Orb speedShows the stress state. Proposed cycle: Calm 6 s, Recovering 5 s, Rising 4 s, Stressed and Critical 3 s. Critical never moves faster than Stressed, so the app adds no pressure.
Breathing orbSets the pace. It expands on the breath in and contracts on the breath out, and slows as heart rate drops.
Result sparkleAppears once, then holds still.
Reduced motionThe orb stops moving, and the breath is shown as text.

Movement answers an action or carries meaning. Nothing moves only to look busy. The speeds are proposed and need testing.
